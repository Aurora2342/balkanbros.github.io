About the Bot

Balkan Bros Bot is meticulously designed to serve communities of all sizes, providing server administrators and staff with powerful, yet easy-to-use moderation tools. It allows you to instantly mute members causing disruptions, ban repeat offenders, and issue warnings while keeping a detailed log of all moderation actions. Every feature is optimized to ensure smooth operation, giving your server a safe, welcoming, and well-organized environment. Whether you run a small gaming community, a large social hub, or a professional team server, Balkan Bros Bot enhances your management capabilities while reducing manual work, allowing moderators to focus on engagement and community growth.

Slash Commands

  • /ping — Check the bot's latency and ensure it is fully operational, providing immediate feedback to moderators.
  • /mute @user [reason] — Temporarily prevent a user from sending messages or speaking in voice channels, perfect for controlling disruptive behavior without permanent consequences.
  • /unmute @user — Restore a previously muted member's permissions, making it easy to manage temporary restrictions efficiently.
  • /ban @user [reason] — Permanently remove troublesome members from your server with a clear record of the action, helping maintain a secure and respectful environment.
  • /warn @user [reason] — Issue official warnings to users, keeping track of their infractions over time, which can be referenced for future moderation decisions.
  • /warns @user — Display a complete list of warnings a user has received, giving staff a comprehensive overview of behavioral history.
